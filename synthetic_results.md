# Synthetic Pretraining Dataset

The synthetic data generator has completed successfully. 

- **Total Images Generated**: 5,000 (saved in `./data/synthetic/`)
- **Labels Mapping**: Saved in `./data/synthetic/synthetic_labels.csv`

The images were generated using MNIST digits and augmented with randomized spacing, slight rotation, elastic/warp distortions, and brightness/contrast jitter to mimic natural handwritten 10-digit phone numbers.
